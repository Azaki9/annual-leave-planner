Annual Leave Planner

Set a period, mark your days off, then export the totals.

Weekend

Click a day to mark it, click it again to clear it. Drag across days to mark several. Shift-click to fill every working day between two days. Keys 1 to 5 pick a type, 0 picks the eraser. Weekends are never counted.
